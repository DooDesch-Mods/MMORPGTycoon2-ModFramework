[size=6][b]MMORPG Tycoon 2 Vortex Extension[/b][/size]

🛟 [b]Need help or found a bug?[/b] Get support at [url=https://support.doodesch.de/modframework]support.doodesch.de/modframework[/url].

[b]MMORPG Tycoon 2 Vortex Extension[/b] lets Vortex manage MMORPG Tycoon 2. Vortex finds your Steam game and puts the MT2 Mod Framework, its mods and classic mods where the game reads them.

[size=5][b]New in 0.2.0[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Uses Vortex's normal deployment. Your staging folder must be on the same drive as the game.
[*]Installs classic mods (mods without a [font=Courier New]mod.json[/font]), not only scenery packs.
[*]All Vortex profiles share one framework profile: [font=Courier New]MMORPG Tycoon 2\mt2fw-profile[/font].
[*]Updating from 0.1.x? Purge first, see [b]Updating from 0.1.x[/b] below.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Windows x64 and the Steam version of MMORPG Tycoon 2 on a [url=https://github.com/DooDesch-Mods/MMORPGTycoon2-ModFramework/blob/main/docs/compatibility.md]supported game build[/url].
[*]Vortex 2.8.2 or newer, with its staging folder on the same drive as the game.
[*]The Runtime from [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mmorpgtycoon2/mods/25]MT2 Mod Framework[/url], a separate download.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game. In Vortex, open [b]Extensions[/b], drop in this ZIP and restart Vortex.
[*]Manage [b]MMORPG Tycoon 2[/b].
[*]Install the [b]Runtime ZIP[/b] from the MT2 Mod Framework page. Enable it and deploy.
[*]Install your mods, enable them and deploy.
[*]Start the game through Steam. [b]F10[/b] opens the Mods menu.
[/list]
Framework mods go to [font=Courier New]mt2fw-profile\mods[/font], classic mods to [font=Courier New]mt2fw-profile\game-data\YOUR-STEAM-ID\mod[/font]. Your worlds and settings are stored in [font=Courier New]mt2fw-profile\game-data[/font].

[size=5][b]Classic mods[/b][/size]
Classic mods load only while the Runtime is enabled and deployed. Without it, Vortex shows a warning after you install or deploy one. Keep Steam signed in when you install a classic mod, so the extension finds your account folder. Classic mod support is tested with a test archive only. MT2 Mod Manager packages, character cards and tools are not recognized; install those by hand.

[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Steam version of the game only.
[*]Installed the Framework by hand or with the old helper? Remove it with that helper before you switch to Vortex.
[*]A game update can need a Runtime update. Until then the game starts without mods and with its normal save folder. Your mods and worlds stay on disk.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Updating from 0.1.x[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Before you update the extension, open [b]Mods[/b] in Vortex and click [b]Purge[/b].
[*]To keep your worlds, copy [font=Courier New]MMORPG Tycoon 2\.mt2fw-vortex\profiles\vortex-PROFILE-ID\game-data[/font] to [font=Courier New]MMORPG Tycoon 2\mt2fw-profile\game-data[/font].
[*]Reinstall classic scenery packs.
[/list]
Already updated without purging? Close the game and delete [font=Courier New]WSOCK32.dll[/font], [font=Courier New]MT2Framework.dll[/font], [font=Courier New]mt2fw.json[/font], [font=Courier New]framework-package.json[/font], the [font=Courier New]mt2fw[/font] folder, [font=Courier New].mt2fw-vortex-owner.json[/font] and [font=Courier New].mt2fw-copy-deployment.json[/font] from the game folder. Then deploy.

[size=5][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Moved the game folder? Set the new location in Vortex, reinstall the Runtime (right-click, [b]Reinstall[/b]) and deploy.
[*]The Vortex log lists every install step with the prefix [font=Courier New]MT2:[/font].
[/list]

[size=5][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Close the game, disable the mods and purge in Vortex. Your worlds stay in [font=Courier New]mt2fw-profile\game-data[/font]. Then remove the extension and restart Vortex.
