[size=4][b]Early Beta 26[/b][/size]
Move selections, copy scenery and buildings, and group scenery together. Undo and Redo work with these shared edits, so you can change your plans together.

Beta 26 also fixes desyncs when only one player opens building info and reduces stalls during Group edits.

[b]This is an early beta. Expect bugs, desyncs and unfinished features.[/b] Modular construction, dungeons and general management actions are not yet available in the new shared simulation. Start with a small world and keep backups of your saves.

[size=4][b]Start playing[/b][/size]
Both players need [b]Beta 26[/b], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mmorpgtycoon2/mods/25][b]MT2 Mod Framework Runtime 0.5.1[/b][/url] and the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/site/mods/2465][b]MMORPG Tycoon 2 Vortex Extension[/b][/url]. Install the Runtime, not the developer SDK.

[list=1]
[*]Close the game and install the Framework Runtime and Vortex Extension.
[*]Add the Multiplayer ZIP to Vortex for MMORPG Tycoon 2.
[*]Enable the mod, deploy, then start through Steam.
[*]Open the Multiplayer desktop icon, or use Mods... > Multiplayer > Open.
[*]Host a lobby and create or load a small world, or join your friend's lobby. Wait until both players are ready before building.
[/list]
