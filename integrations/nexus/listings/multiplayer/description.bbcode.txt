[size=4][b]Early Beta[/b][/size]
Manage supported staff and advertising actions together, change release plans and Inn service prices, and continue building after a shared autosave. Read your installed version's release notes for its changes and requirements.

[b]This is an early beta. Expect bugs, desyncs and unfinished features.[/b] Some management actions, connected travel services, modular construction and dungeons remain incomplete. Start with a small world and keep backups of your saves.

[size=4][b]Start playing[/b][/size]
Both players need the [b]same Multiplayer version[/b] and the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mmorpgtycoon2/mods/25][b]MT2 Mod Framework Runtime[/b][/url] required by that version's release notes. For installation through Vortex, use the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/site/mods/2465][b]MMORPG Tycoon 2 Vortex Extension[/b][/url]. Choose the Runtime download.

[list=1]
[*]Close the game and install the Framework Runtime and Vortex Extension.
[*]Add the Multiplayer ZIP to Vortex for MMORPG Tycoon 2.
[*]Enable the mod, deploy, then start through Steam.
[*]Open the Multiplayer desktop icon, or use Mods... > Multiplayer > Open.
[*]Host a lobby and create or load a small world, or join your friend's lobby. Wait until both players are ready before building.
[/list]
