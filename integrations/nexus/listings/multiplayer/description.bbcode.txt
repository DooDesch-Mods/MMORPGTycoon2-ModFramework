[size=4][b]Early Beta 25[/b][/size]
Rejoin after restarting the game and continue building. Beta 25 fixes simulation drift after rejoining and restores world selection after players join.

The new simulation is still in testing. [b]Groups, modular buildings, dungeons and management actions are not yet available.[/b] Other gameplay scenarios and performance remain under test. Start with a small world and keep backups of your saves.

[size=4][b]Start playing[/b][/size]
Both players need [b]Beta 25[/b], [b]MT2 Mod Framework Runtime 0.5.0[/b] and the [b]MMORPG Tycoon 2 Vortex Extension[/b]. Install the Runtime, not the developer SDK.

[list=1]
[*]Close the game and install the Framework Runtime and Vortex Extension.
[*]Add the Multiplayer ZIP to Vortex for MMORPG Tycoon 2.
[*]Enable the mod, deploy, then start through Steam.
[*]Open the Multiplayer desktop icon, or use Mods... > Multiplayer > Open.
[*]Host a lobby and create or load a small world, or join your friend's lobby. Wait until both players are ready before building.
[/list]
